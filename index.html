Abstract

Cross-view object correspondence identifies the same object across views. However, existing methods treat this as a frame-level matching problem, necessitating a query mask at every frame. Furthermore, without modeling temporal context, they suffer from severe ambiguity under appearance changes or occlusions. Therefore, we introduce cross-view temporal object correspondence, which requires preserving object identity across both views and time from a query mask. To address this, we propose XTrack, a streamlined framework which repurposes a pretrained video DiT with semantic and spatiotemporal priors for RGB-to-mask latent transport, jointly modeling cross-view matching and temporal tracking. However, naive video DiTs do not guarantee accurate cross-view grounding and often attend to semantically similar yet incorrect objects across views. Thus, we introduce focal cross-view attention alignment, hard negative conditioning, and local context crop strategies to ensure reliable grounding. Finally, beyond these architectural enhancements, we also tackle the lack of dense annotation by introducing a pseudo-label curation pipeline that converts sparse Ego-Exo4D annotations into dense supervision to enable effective video-level training. Our highly competitive performances validate the effectiveness of XTrack as a streamlined framework, ensuring robust temporal consistency and identity preservation.

Motivation

Given a first-frame query mask in one view, the task is to recover temporally coherent masks of the same object in both views. Frame-level matchers must be paired with a tracker: (a) Track-then-Match propagates the query mask and matches it per frame, (b) Match-then-Track matches once and tracks each view independently. Both break under disappearance and viewpoint change. (c) XTrack models cross-view correspondence and temporal dynamics jointly in a single video DiT.

Method

Overall architecture. The ego and exo videos are placed side by side in a single frame and fed to a pretrained video DiT, which transports RGB latents to mask latents for both views at once.
RGB-to-mask flow
  • Flow matching from the RGB latent to the mask latent, keeping the video DiT priors.
Unified ego-exo tokens
  • Ego and exo frames are placed side by side in one input, so cross-view matching and within-view tracking happen in the same attention.
Focal attention alignment
  • Cross-view attention is supervised to land on the query object.
Hard negative conditioning
  • Run the same videos with the correct query mask and with another object's mask; a margin loss keeps the two predictions apart.
Local context crop
  • Object crop tokens with original RoPE positions for small objects.
Two-stage training
  • Image-level cycle-consistency pretraining, then video fine-tuning on curated pseudo-labels.

Pseudo-Label Curation

Ego-Exo4D provides 30 FPS video but object masks only at 1 FPS, which is too sparse to train a video model, and plain SAM2 propagation is unreliable in the ego view. Our curation pipeline propagates the sparse annotations and verifies them across views and within each view, turning the 1 FPS annotations into 30 FPS pseudo-labels. The resulting dense, verified labels are what XTrack is fine-tuned on at the video level.

a
Bidirectional propagation
Forward and backward SAM2 between anchors; chunks where the two propagations disagree are selected for verification.
b
Cross-view geometric verification
VGGT similarity to the exo view selects one of the two candidates.
c
Within-view semantic verification
DINOv3 similarity to the first-frame ego anchor discards chunks that are still wrong.

Cross-view geometric verification

Exo query (left), the forward (red) and backward (blue) ego candidates, and the candidate selected by VGGT similarity to the exo view (right).

Within-view semantic verification

Geometric verification only selects between the forward and backward candidates, so it cannot recover a chunk where both candidates are wrong. Semantic verification complements it: the selected candidate is compared with the first-frame ego anchor, and chunks with low similarity are discarded.
